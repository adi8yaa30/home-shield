Where Home Shield Fits

Every space asks something different of a frame. These are the installations our sections are specified for most often.

Windows

Casement, sliding and tilt & turn openings sized to the wall rather than to a stock frame.

Living Rooms

Wide glazed spans that hold daylight without letting in the street noise behind it.

Office Spaces

Repeatable window runs for commercial floors, where consistency across bays matters more than any single frame.

Sliding Doors

Heavy sashes on reinforced tracks, built to keep sliding smoothly after thousands of cycles.

Study Rooms

Quiet rooms where the seal matters most — acoustic performance without shutting out the light.

Bathrooms & Utility

Constant damp where timber swells and metal pits — frames that take years of steam and splashes without rot, rust or repainting.

Built For The Conditions

The same section performs differently depending on where it is installed. These are the conditions we specify against.

01Coastal
Salt air corrodes metal frames and hardware. uPVC does not rust, and the sections carry no exposed steel.
02High-rise
Wind load rises with every floor. Reinforcement is specified for the opening size, not assumed from the range.
03Hot and dry
Sustained sun and heat without fade, warp or the finish chalking off the surface.
04Monsoon
Drainage detailing carries driven rain back out of the frame instead of into the wall.
05Dense urban
Multi-chamber sections and proper glazing beads cut the traffic noise that reaches the room.
06Dust-heavy
Smooth surfaces and tight seals mean a wipe-down, not a maintenance routine.
