About Home Shield

We build uPVC profile systems for the people who have to stand behind them — the fabricator, the architect, the builder, and the family inside.

Built on one stubborn idea

A window is not a finish. It is the line between a home and the weather, and it is expected to hold that line for decades without being thought about.

Home Shield exists because too many profiles are specified on price and replaced on regret. We engineer sections that are dimensionally honest, consistent batch to batch, and predictable on the fabrication floor — so what leaves our line performs the same way on site as it does on paper.

Every section we make is a promise kept to the architect who drew it, the fabricator who cut it, and the family who lives behind it.

How We Work

  1. 01

    Specify

    We start from the opening, the exposure and the loads — then recommend the series and reinforcement that suit it.

  2. 02

    Extrude

    Profiles run against fixed tolerances with in-line checks, so what ships matches the approved sample.

  3. 03

    Fabricate

    Sections are built to weld and machine cleanly on standard equipment, with hardware compatibility confirmed up front.

  4. 04

    Stand Behind It

    Documentation, support and supply continuity for the life of the project — not just until the invoice clears.

Tested, Not Assumed

A profile earns its place in the range by surviving the checks below. Anything that fails one does not ship.

  • Wall thickness

    Section walls are measured against the approved drawing, not against the last batch that happened to pass.

  • Weld strength

    Corner welds are pulled to failure so the joint, not the frame, defines the safety margin.

  • UV and colour hold

    Accelerated exposure checks that whites stay white and laminates keep their depth.

  • Water and air tightness

    Glazing and drainage detailing are tested wet, under pressure, in the closed position.

  • Hardware cycling

    Hinges, locks and rollers are run through repeated open-close cycles on the finished sash.

  • Batch consistency

    Dimensions are logged per run, so a section ordered next year matches the one cut today.
